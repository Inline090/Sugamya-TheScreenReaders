# Competitive evidence and claims discipline

Researched 24 October 2026 using current primary material. “Not verified” means the inspected sources did not establish that capability, **not that the product lacks it**. Sugamya is not the first accessibility agent, scanner, alt-text reviewer, runtime remediation service, or human approval workflow.

## Defensible shipped difference

**A small, inspectable operator-owned change-control loop**, not a broad superiority claim:

1. One installed public-service task drives an explicit finding-priority heuristic.
2. Exact reviewed attribute manifest reaches visitors through an owner-controlled proxy without origin source patching.
3. Minimal optional AI text drafting has explicit payload consent; semantic meaning remains held for a human.
4. Fresh-browser canary and ordinary-visitor gates withhold bad changes and restore serving pointers on failure.
5. Original public observations, decisions, manifests, tests and failure events are replayable and connected to honest coverage/effort denominators.

These mechanisms are demonstrated together in this bounded OSS MVP. The evidence does not establish that no commercial or open-source system offers the same combination. The originality claim is an implementation/delivery pattern aimed at constrained operators, not invention of runtime remediation or human review.

## Application claims checked before reuse

- Mishra et al.'s preprint actually reports **86% recall vs 36% axe-core** on its report-derived benchmark, **56% precision**, 250 page-criterion records and 78 positives. Implementing 40 workers is not performance proof on 40 criteria; positive reference cases covered 15 criteria. This is external detection research, **not Sugamya accuracy or repair evidence**. [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09379), [2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09379)
- Original team's 26-page/767-instance scan has no underlying observations in the supplied workspace; **not repeated as a measured result**.
- Original WebAIM/legal/draft-regulation statistics are not needed for our fixture proof and are **not reused as verified facts or legal advice**.
- A whole-category statement that “overlays only hide barriers” is too broad: some owner-installed tools really change accessible DOM. We instead specify the behavior, visitor reach, ordering, limits and control evidence of our selected route.
